# Retail Sales Analytics with DuckDB

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end retail analytics workflow using **Python**, **Pandas**, **DuckDB**, and **SQL**. It transforms raw transactional data into a validated and feature-enriched analytical dataset, then uses business-focused SQL queries to evaluate customer behavior, geographic performance, product classifications, profitability, discounting, shipping, and time-based trends.

The completed workflow currently includes:

1. Data preprocessing and validation with Python and Pandas
2. Feature engineering with DuckDB and SQL
3. Descriptive business analytics with SQL

Planned stages include interpretable predictive modeling with multiple linear regression and an interactive Power BI dashboard. Each stage builds on the preceding work, creating a reproducible progression from raw data to decision-ready business insights.

## Business Metrics and Definitions

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Revenue (Sales)** | The total dollar amount generated from products sold. The dataset uses `sales` to represent revenue. |
| **Profit** | The financial gain or loss associated with a sales line. Positive values represent gains, while negative values represent losses. |
| **Profit Margin** | The percentage of revenue retained as profit. For aggregated groups, it is calculated as `SUM(profit) / SUM(sales)`. |
| **Discount** | The percentage reduction applied to a product's original selling price. |
| **Quantity** | The number of product units purchased on a sales line. |
| **Average Order Value** | Total sales divided by the number of distinct orders. |
| **Fulfillment Days** | An engineered feature measuring the number of days between the order date and ship date. |
| **Customer Lifetime Sales** | An engineered feature representing the total recorded revenue generated by a customer. |
| **Customer Span Days** | An engineered feature measuring the number of days between a customer's first and most recent recorded purchases. |
| **Customer Tier** | An engineered classification that groups customers according to lifetime sales. |
| **Order Year** | The year extracted from `order_date` for annual analysis. |
| **Order Month** | The month extracted from `order_date` for seasonal analysis. |

Because a single order may contain multiple product lines, order-based measures use `COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)`. Aggregated profit margins are calculated from total profit and total sales rather than by averaging row-level margins.

## Business Questions

The project investigates questions such as:

- Which customers and customer groups generate the greatest financial value?
- Which regions and states combine strong sales with sustainable profitability?
- Which product categories and subcategories perform best or generate losses?
- At what discount levels does profitability begin to deteriorate?
- How have sales, profit, order volume, and average order value changed over time?
- Which months account for the greatest business activity?
- How does fulfillment speed differ across shipping modes?

## Key Findings from Business Analysis

- Annual sales increased by approximately **51.41%** from 2014 to 2017, while profit increased by approximately **88.60%**. Growth was driven primarily by increased order volume because average order value declined over the same period.
- Elite customers represented approximately **10% of the customer base** but contributed **30.88% of total sales** and **42.66% of total profit**.
- The West generated the highest regional sales, profit, and profit margin. At the state level, high revenue did not consistently indicate strong profitability; Texas generated the third-highest sales but the largest state-level loss.
- Technology led all categories in sales and profit. Furniture generated substantial revenue but achieved a profit margin of only **2.49%**, with Tables and Bookcases contributing to its weak performance.
- Aggregate profitability became negative at the **30% discount level**, and every sales line discounted by **45% or more** generated a loss. The effect of discounting varied across product categories, supporting category-specific discount policies.
- September through December generated approximately **51.62% of total sales**, indicating a meaningful concentration of activity late in the calendar year.
- Standard Class represented approximately **59.77% of orders** and averaged **5.00 fulfillment days**. Same Day was least common and averaged **0.05 days**.

## Data-Quality Decision

Notebook 01 identified cases in which a single `product_id` was associated with multiple `product_name` values. Without access to the original database, supporting business documentation, or relevant stakeholders, the correct identifier-name relationships could not be determined.

The original identifiers were preserved because assigning replacements would introduce assumptions that could not be verified. Individual-product comparisons were therefore excluded from the business analysis. Product performance is evaluated at the independently validated `category` and `sub_category` levels, so the identifier discrepancies do not affect the reported product-classification findings.

## Project Workflow

### Notebook 01 — Data Preprocessing

The first notebook establishes whether the raw retail dataset can be trusted for downstream analysis.

Tasks include:

- Importing and inspecting the dataset
- Resolving character-encoding issues
- Standardizing column names
- Converting data types
- Detecting missing values and duplicate records
- Validating numeric ranges and chronological relationships
- Verifying categorical and identifier relationships
- Documenting unresolved product-identifier inconsistencies without altering the source values
- Exporting a clean UTF-8 dataset for DuckDB

### Notebook 02 — DuckDB Feature Engineering

The second notebook enriches the validated dataset with reusable analytical features while preserving the original variables.

Engineered features include:

- `fulfillment_days`
- `profit_margin`
- `order_year`
- `order_month`
- `customer_lifetime_sales`
- `customer_span_days`
- `customer_tier`

DuckDB views separate the base data from its engineered representation, supporting reproducibility and clear data lineage. The resulting feature-enriched dataset is exported for downstream analysis.

### Notebook 03 — Business SQL Analysis

The third notebook uses SQL to answer stakeholder-oriented business questions. Each analysis follows a consistent structure: **Question, Objective, Variables, SQL Query, Results, and Key Insights**.

The notebook contains five analytical sections:

1. **Customer Analysis** — top customers, segment performance, and customer-tier profitability
2. **Geographic Analysis** — regional performance, highest-selling states, and loss-generating states
3. **Product Classification Analysis** — category and subcategory sales, profitability, and financial losses
4. **Sales and Profitability Analysis** — discount-level performance, profitability status, and loss-generating category-discount combinations
5. **Shipping and Time Analysis** — annual performance, monthly patterns, and order-level fulfillment performance

The notebook concludes with consolidated findings and business recommendations.

### Notebook 04 — Predictive Analytics *(Planned)*

The planned fourth notebook will use multiple linear regression to examine whether selected business variables can predict a defined financial outcome and to interpret the factors associated with that outcome.

The proposed workflow includes:

- Defining the prediction target and unit of analysis
- Selecting and encoding predictors
- Splitting training and test data
- Fitting a multiple linear regression model
- Evaluating performance using R², RMSE, and MAE
- Checking model assumptions and limitations
- Interpreting coefficients and business implications

### Interactive Power BI Dashboard *(Planned)*

The planned dashboard will translate the most decision-relevant findings into an interactive business intelligence report. It is expected to include executive KPIs, sales and profitability trends, customer and geographic insights, shipping metrics, and filters for year, region, state, category, and customer segment.
